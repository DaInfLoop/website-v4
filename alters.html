headmates / system information

foreword

icymi, we're a plural system of 8 headmates. this page simply exists to describe each of us and just give brief introductions.

each visual depiction listed here is only really meant to give a mental image of that headmate.

each headmate listed here is not an OC. we are all people, please don't treat us as characters.

rana

pronouns: any/all

rana is the person you're most likely to encounter if you talk to us.

usually they're quiet and don't like to be talked to that often. contradictingly however, they also ramble a lot. like.. a lot. once they've started, it's pretty hard for them to stop.

ari

pronouns: they/she

ari is very energetic and very easy to get on along with. ari's also very childish.

ari prefers to be referred to as "ari", but is okay with they/she pronouns when the situation doesn't really allow for ari's name to be used.
ari also speaks very simple english, more or less just caveperson speak.

nora

pronouns: it/its

nora is very much a "straight to the point" person. it doesn't really care about how things are done, as long as they're done.

nora originally was thought of to be a persecutor: someone who exists purely just to hurt the system. however, as time went on, it learnt to control itself, and now helps the system in need whenever it can.
it can however be somewhat short-tempered, and can get annoyed at people quite easily if they attempt to annoy the system.

athena

pronouns: she/her

athena is there to protect the rest of the system when they need it.

not much is really known about athena. she only really appears when the system is in dire need of help, and not at all apart from that.

zephyr

pronouns: fae/faer

zephyr is quite an unknown alter.

fae prefer to work in silence a lot of the time. fae won't talk unless fae absolutely has to, but even then fae try to speak as little as possible.
not much is known about fae, not even to faerself.

crimson

pronouns: they/them

crimson is yet another "straight to the point" alter. however, unlike nora, they don't necessarily take action.

crimson finds societal norms weird: they question a lot about how the world works. they also live by the phrase "you only live once", feeling like just doing anything for the sake of it.

astral

pronouns: he/him

astral is somewhat a teenager who represents our pre-plural self: acts silly but can be very serious. sometimes

astral prefers to be with other people most of the time, and while extroverted, he finds it hard to start conversations most of the time.
